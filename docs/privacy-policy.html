Kundoluk Teacher Companion Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026. This policy applies to version 0.12.1.

Kundoluk Teacher Companion is an independent, unofficial browser extension. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Kundoluk or the Ministry of Education.

Data processed on your device

The extension reads supported Kundoluk page labels and lesson context to provide English interface help and prepare lesson topics and homework. It processes Excel workbooks you explicitly import. It stores imported workbook names and lesson text, class and subject assignments, captured lesson schedules, teacher-confirmed lesson progress, and interface preferences in Chrome extension storage on your device.

No collection by the developer

The extension does not send this data to the developer or third parties. It contains no analytics, advertising, remote translation service, payment system, or developer-operated server. It does not read passwords, cookies, or credential input values. It does not export student rosters or grades.

Your school platform

The extension can fill topic and homework fields after your confirmation. It does not press the school's Save button. If you save the prepared content yourself, Kundoluk processes it under its own policies. Your normal browser and Kundoluk connections are separate from this extension.

Retention and removal

Saved data remains in this Chrome profile until you remove it through the extension controls, clear the extension's storage, or uninstall the extension. It is not synced or backed up by the extension. Keep original workbooks as backups. Removing a saved workbook does not remove independent class assignments or progress records; uninstalling removes extension storage.

Permissions

The storage permission retains your plans and preferences locally. The content script runs only on https://kundoluk.edu.gov.kg/mon* to provide the teacher companion.

Changes and contact

The extension uses locally processed data only to provide its stated teacher-interface and lesson-preparation features. It does not sell data, use it for advertising, transfer it for unrelated purposes or lending decisions, or allow developer access to your stored plans. This use follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Any future change to data handling will be reflected in this policy. For questions, contact the publisher using the support contact on the Chrome Web Store listing.
