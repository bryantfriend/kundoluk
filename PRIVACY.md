# Kundoluk Teacher Companion Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026. Applies to version 0.12.1.

This is an independent, unofficial extension, not affiliated with or endorsed by Kundoluk or the Ministry of Education.

## Data processed locally

The extension reads supported teacher-interface labels and lesson context to provide English help and prepare topics and homework. It processes Excel workbooks you explicitly import. Imported workbook names and lesson text, class/subject assignments, captured schedules, teacher-confirmed progress and interface preferences are saved in Chrome extension storage on your device.

## No developer collection

The extension sends none of this data to the developer or third parties. It has no analytics, ads, remote translation service, payment system or developer-operated server. It does not read passwords, cookies or credential input values, and does not export student rosters or grades.

## Your school platform

It fills topic and homework fields after your confirmation and never presses Save. When you manually save content, Kundoluk processes it under its own policies.

## Retention and removal

Data remains in this Chrome profile until removed through extension controls, clearing extension storage or uninstalling. The extension does not sync or back it up. Keep original workbooks. Removing a workbook does not remove independent class assignments or progress records; uninstalling removes extension storage.

## Permissions and limited use

The storage permission retains plans and preferences locally. The content script runs only on `https://kundoluk.edu.gov.kg/mon*`. Locally processed data serves only the stated teacher-interface and lesson-preparation features. The extension does not sell data, use it for advertising, transfer it for unrelated purposes or lending decisions, or allow developer access to stored plans. This use follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Contact

Use the publisher contact on the Chrome Web Store listing or the project's GitHub issues. Do not post student records, credentials or private workbooks in public issues. Future data-handling changes will be reflected in this policy.
